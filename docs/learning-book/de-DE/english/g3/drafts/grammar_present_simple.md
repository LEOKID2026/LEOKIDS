## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute lernen wir the Present Simple tense.
Positive, negative, and question forms.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Positive:
I play tennis.

Negative:
I do not play tennis.

Question:
Do you play tennis?

---

## 3. Beispiel

A child playing tennis.

I play tennis.

I play tennis.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

Question: how do we say "I play tennis"?

I play tennis

Answer: I play tennis

---

## 5. Probiere es selbst

A girl playing tennis.

She plays tennis.

Versuch, es selbst zu lösen.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam den Weg und die Antwort.

---

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

Exercise: a girl playing tennis.

Die Lösung:

She plays tennis.

She plays tennis.

Well done! After she, he, or it, we add s to the verb — plays. After I, we don't add s — I play.

---

## 7. Lass uns üben!

Now you know the Present Simple — positive, negative, and question forms.
In der Übung you'll find I play tennis and Do you play?
