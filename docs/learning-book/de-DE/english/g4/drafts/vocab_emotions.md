## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute lernen wir etwas über emotions using "They."
They feel excited — they are excited.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Muster:

They feel excited.

They feel happy.

---

## 3. Beispiel

Children before a trip.

They feel excited.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

Frage: Wie sagst du das?

They feel excited

Answer: They feel excited

---

## 5. Probiere es selbst

Children at the end of a rainy day.

They feel sad.

Probiere es selbst.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam die Antwort und den Lösungsweg.

---

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

Exercise: "They feel sad."

"They" is plural — after "they" we do not add s to the verb feel.

"Sad" describes how they feel.

So the full sentence is: They feel sad.

---

## 7. Lass uns üben!

Now you will describe emotions using "They."
In the practice, you'll find: They feel excited.
