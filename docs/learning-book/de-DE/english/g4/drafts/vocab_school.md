## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

Exercise: "The Schülerinnen und Schüler listen to the teacher."

"The Schülerinnen und Schüler" is plural — after a plural subject we do not add s to the verb listen.

"Listen to" means to pay attention to something.

So the full sentence is: The Schülerinnen und Schüler listen to the teacher.
