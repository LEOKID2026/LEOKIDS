## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute werden wir strengthen the patterns I am, You are — and add He is and She is.
We already learned I am — now let's add more pronouns.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Patterns:
I am — I
You are — you
He is — he
She is — she
Beispiel:
She is happy.
She is happy.

---

## 3. Beispiel

A happy girl.
She is happy.
She is happy.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

How do we say "she is happy"?
She is happy

---

## 5. Probiere es selbst

A sad boy.
He is sad
Versuch, es selbst zu lösen.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam den Weg und die Antwort.

---

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

The question: a sad boy — in English?
He means he.
sad means sad.
The answer is: He is sad.

---

## 7. Lass uns üben!

Now you know I am, You are, He is, She is.
In der Übung you'll find She is happy and He is sad.
