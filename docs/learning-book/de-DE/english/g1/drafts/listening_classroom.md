## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute lernen wir, listen to short instructions in English in the classroom.
One step at a time — first we listen.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Stand up. — Get up.
Sit down. — Sit down.
Open your book. — Open your book.
Close your book. — Close your book.

---

## 3. Beispiel

The teacher says: "Stand up."
We stand up — that's the instruction.
The teacher says: "Sit down."
We sit down.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

Was machen wir do when we hear "Open your book"?
Open means to open.
Book means a book.
So we open our book.
Open your book means: open the book.

---

## 5. Probiere es selbst

Was machen wir do when we hear "Close your book"?
Versuch, es selbst zu lösen.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam den Weg und die Antwort.

---

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

Was machen wir do when we hear "Close your book"?
Close means to close.
Book means a book.
So we close the book.
Close your book means: close the book.
