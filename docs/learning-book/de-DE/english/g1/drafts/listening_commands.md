## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute lernen wir more instructions — with words we've already learned.
We listen — and choose the picture of the action or object.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Point to the door. — Point to the door.
Show me your pen. — Show me your pen.
Listen. — Listen.
Look. — Look.

---

## 3. Beispiel

We hear: "Show me your pen."
We look for pen — a pen — and show it.
We hear: "Point to the door."
We point to door — a door.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

Which picture matches "Point to the door"?
door — a door.
We choose a picture of a door.
picture of a door

---

## 5. Probiere es selbst

Was machen wir do when we hear "Look."?
Versuch, es selbst zu lösen.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam den Weg und die Antwort.

---

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

Was machen wir do when we hear "Look."?
Look.
This word means: look.
We look — that's the action.
Look means: look.
