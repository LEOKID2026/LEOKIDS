## 1. Was lernen wir?

Heute lernen wir two short patterns in English.
They help us say who we are.

---

## 2. Einfache Erklärung

Two fixed patterns:
I am — starts a sentence about myself.
You are — starts a sentence about the person we're talking to.
Then we add a word like happy or sad.
Beispiel:
I am happy — I am happy.
You are sad — You are sad.

---

## 3. Beispiel

A happy child.
I am happy
I am happy.

---

## 4. Lass uns gemeinsam lösen

How do we say "I am happy" in English?
We start with I am.
We add happy.
I am happy

## 5. Probiere es selbst

How do we say "You are sad" in English?
Versuch, es selbst zu lösen.
Auf der nächsten Seite prüfen wir gemeinsam den Weg und die Antwort.

## 6. Lass uns gemeinsam prüfen

How do we say "You are sad" in English?
We start with You are.
We add sad.
You are sad
You are sad means: you are sad.
