## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today noi strengthen divisione — uguale sharing con no remainder — using multi-passaggio breaking apart e checking.
Noi ask: come molti times fa divisor fit in dividend? E noi controllare ogni passaggio.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

144,000 ÷ 12 — break apart in passaggi (large numeri):
Passaggio UN: 12 × 10,000 = 120,000 → remainder 24,000
Passaggio B: 12 × 2,000 = 24,000 → remainder 0
Summary: 10,000 + 2,000 = 12,000
144,000 ÷ 12 = 12,000
Controllare: 12,000 × 12 = 144,000

---

## 3. Esempio visuale / concreto

360,000 ÷ 15 — break apart:
15 × 20,000 = 300,000 → remainder 60,000
15 × 4,000 = 60,000 → remainder 0
20,000 + 4,000 = 24,000
360,000 ÷ 15 = 24,000
Controllare: 24,000 × 15 = 360,000

---

## 4. Risolviamo insieme

Calculate: 96,000 ÷ 8 =?
- 8 × 10,000 = 80,000
- Remainder: 96,000 − 80,000 = 16,000
8 × 2,000 = 16,000 remainder: 0.
Passaggio 3 — controllare: (10,000 + 2,000) × 8 = 12,000 × 8 = 96,000

## 6. Errore comune — attenzione!

Calculate: 1,440 ÷ 12
12 × 100 = 1,200 → remainder 1,440 − 1,200 = 240.
12 × 20 = 240 → remainder 0.
100 + 20 = 120
Controllare: 120 × 12 = 1,440
risposta: 120
