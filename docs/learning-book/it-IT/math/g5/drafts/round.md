## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today we'll imparare a round intero numeri a nearest dieci, hundred, thousand, e dieci-thousand.
Rounding helps tu estimate risposte in math e fare quick mental math.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

Passaggi per rounding:
Look at digit a right di place you're rounding a
Se it's 0, 1, 2, 3, o 4 — round down (stay stesso)
Se it's 5, 6, 7, 8, o 9 — round up (addizionare 1)
Rounding a nearest dieci:
Look at ones
Rounding a nearest hundred:
Look at tens
Rounding a nearest thousand:
Look at hundreds
Rounding a nearest dieci-thousand:
Look at thousands
38,750 a nearest dieci-thousand:
Thousands digit 8 → round up → 40,000
