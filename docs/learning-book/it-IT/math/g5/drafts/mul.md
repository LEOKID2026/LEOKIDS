## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today we'll imparare moltiplicazione — including due-digit moltiplicazione e breaking-apart strategies.
Multiplication helps con quick math e solving problems.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

Multiplication strategies:
Multiplying by friendly numeri: 25 × 4 = 100
Breaking apart: 25 × 36 = 25 × 30 + 25 × 6 = 750 + 150 = 900
Order doesn't matter: 36 × 25 = 25 × 36
25 × 36:
25 × 30 = 750
25 × 6 = 150
750 + 150 = 900
