## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today we'll imparare a sottrarre due numeri up a 10,000.
Quando lì aren't enough in ones, tens, hundreds, o thousands — borrow da place a left.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

3,002 − 1,487:
Ones: 2 < 7 — borrow da 0 tens → 0 tens, borrow da 3 hundreds → 2 hundreds left, 10 tens, 12 ones.
Ones: 12 − 7 = 5
Tens: 9 − 8 = 1
Hundreds: 2 − 4 — borrow da thousands → 12 hundreds, 2 thousands
Hundreds: 12 − 4 = 8
Thousands: 2 − 1 = 1
3,002 − 1,487 = 1,515
Controllare: 1,515 + 1,487 = 3,002

---

## 3. Esempio visuale / concreto

4,620 − 2,385:
Ones: 0 < 5 → borrow → 10 − 5 = 5
Tens: dopo borrowing uno dieci remains, e it's non enough. Borrow da hundreds → 11 − 8 = 3
Hundreds: dopo borrowing, 5 hundreds remain → 5 − 3 = 2
Thousands: 4 − 2 = 2
4,620 − 2,385 = 2,235

---

## 4. Risolviamo insieme

Work out: 5,412 − 2,178 =?
Ones: 12 − 8 = 4 (borrow da 1 dieci).
Tens: 0 − 7 — borrow → 10 − 7 = 3 (hundreds become 4).
Hundreds: 4 − 1 = 3
Thousands: 5 − 2 = 3
5,412 − 2,178 = 3,234
