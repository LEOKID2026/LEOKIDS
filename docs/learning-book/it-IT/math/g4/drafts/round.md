## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today we'll imparare a round un numero a nearest dieci, hundred, o thousand.
Rounding helps tu estimate risposte e fare quick mental math.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

Passaggi per rounding:
Look at digit a right di place you're rounding a
Se it's 0, 1, 2, 3, o 4 — round down (stay stesso)
Se it's 5, 6, 7, 8, o 9 — round up (addizionare 1)
Rounding a tens:
Look at ones digit
Rounding a hundreds:
Look at tens digit
Rounding a thousands:
Look at hundreds digit
3,847 a nearest dieci:
Ones digit 7 → round up → 3,850
