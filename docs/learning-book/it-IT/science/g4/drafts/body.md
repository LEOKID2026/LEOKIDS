## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today in science noi imparare su respiratory system e digestive system — come corpo gets oxygen e breaks down food.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

Breathing — quando noi breathe, aria enters lungs.
lungs pass oxygen a blood; blood brings oxygen a intero corpo.
blood returns carbon dioxide a lungs, e noi breathe esso out.
Digestion — food follows un path:
mouth chews e moistens food, esophagus carries esso a stomach, stomach mixes esso, e in intestines nutrients sono absorbed in corpo.
Oxygen e nutrients da food help corpo get energia per activity.

---

## 3. Esempio

Dopo running — fast breathing.
corpo needs più oxygen per muscles.
Dopo un meal — digestion works in stomach e intestines.
corpo breaks down bread e vegetables in materiali blood carries.

## 6. Controlliamo insieme

question: cosa happens a food in mouth — e dove fa esso go dopo swallowing?
Scientific explanation:
digestive system starts in mouth.
Teeth chew food e break esso in small pieces.
tongue mixes; saliva softens e starts a break down food.
Dopo swallowing, food passes through pharynx e esophagus a stomach — dove breakdown continues.
Da stomach esso goes a intestines, dove nutrients sono absorbed in blood.
In mouth — chewing e saliva. Dopo swallowing — pharynx, esophagus, stomach, e intestines.
