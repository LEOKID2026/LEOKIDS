## 6. Controlliamo insieme

question: quando tu bend tuo knee — quale parte gives figura? E quale parte moves?
Scientific explanation:
skeleton è rigid structure di corpo — bones give corpo figura e support e allow standing.
Muscles wrap around bones e sono connected a them by tendons.
Quando un muscle contracts — esso pulls bone e causes movement.
Quando bending knee:
thigh bone — stays stable e gives figura.
thigh muscle — contracts e moves lower leg bone.
Skeleton (bone) — gives figura e stability. Muscle — moves bone.
