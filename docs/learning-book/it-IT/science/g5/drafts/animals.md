---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

Reproduction — creating un new generation:
- Eggs — fish, birds, molti reptiles
- Live birth — molti mammals
- Development in stages — insects, per esempio butterfly: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, adult; amphibians, per esempio frog: egg, tadpole, adult
Offspring sono similar a loro genitores — eyes, colorare, figura.
Alcuni traits come da genitores (inherited traits).
Adaptations help survival:
Colorare, size, corpo figura.
Esempio: un brown rabbit in un forest — meno visible a un predator.

## 6. Controlliamo insieme

question: in un butterfly e caterpillar — cosa changes in life stages? E cosa stays stesso species?
Scientific explanation:
Butterflies go through un life cycle in quattro stages:
Egg, allora caterpillar, allora chrysalis, e finally adult — butterfly.
At ogni stage — figura, size, e behavior change un lot.
Cosa stays?
species remains butterfly.
Inside chrysalis un structural change happens; adult butterfly carries inherited traits come wing colorare da suo genitores.
Changes — figura, size, e behavior at ogni stage. Stays — species e inherited traits come wing colorare.
