## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today in science noi imparare come diverso systems in corpo sono coordinated — e come nutrition, sleep, e activity affect health.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

corpo — un system di systems:
- Digestion — breaks food in materiali corpo può use
- Blood e heart — carry oxygen e materiali a intero corpo
- Breathing — brings in oxygen, removes extra gases
- Nerves e brain — coordinate responses
Coordination:
Dopo un meal — digestion breaks down food; blood carries esso; brain feels full.
During activity — muscles work, breathing speeds up, pulse rises.
Balanced nutrition, enough sleep, e physical activity — support tutti systems.

Tutti systems — insieme.

## 6. Controlliamo insieme

question: dopo dinner — quale systems worked so food poteva reach tuo body's cells?
Scientific explanation:
Dopo eating, several systems work insieme:
digestive system — breaks down food in stomach e intestines in absorbable materiali.
blood system — heart pumps e circulates blood; blood carries nutrients a every cell.
respiratory system — brings oxygen che cells need a produce energia da food.
nervous system — coordinates e tells brain quando corpo è full e quando esso needs energia.
Systems che work dopo un meal: digestion, blood, breathing, e nerves — tutti connected.
