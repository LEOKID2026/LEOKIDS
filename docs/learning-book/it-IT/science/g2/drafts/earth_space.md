## 2. Spiegazione semplice

During year lì sono seasons:
Summer — hot, long days, lots di sun
Winter — cooler, sometimes rain
Spring — starts a warm up, flowers bloom
Autumn — gets cooler, leaves fall
sun — un star in sky che gives luce e calore.
During day — sun shines e warms us.
At night — noi fare non see sun in sky; moon e stars appear con weaker luce.
Seasons change — but sun è always main source di luce during day.
