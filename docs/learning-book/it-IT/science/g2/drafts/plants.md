## 2. Spiegazione semplice

UN plant's growth cycle:
Seed — small, found in fruit o in un packet
Germination — seed opens, un root e stem come out
Pianta — green leaves, pianta grows
UN pianta needs sun, acqua, e soil.
Senza acqua — seed fa non sprout o pianta wilts.
