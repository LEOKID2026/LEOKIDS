## 1. Cosa stiamo imparando?

Today in science noi imparare su health e good habits — come a keep nostro corpo strong e happy.

---

## 2. Spiegazione semplice

UN healthy corpo needs good habits:
Hygiene — washing hands prima eating, brushing teeth in morning e evening
Varied food e drink — fruits, vegetables, e acqua give corpo energia
Activity — running, playing, walking — strong muscles
Sleep — rest at night — corpo recovers
Quando noi keep questi habits — noi feel better.

---

## 3. Esempio

Noa woke up in morning, washed sua hands, brushed sua teeth, e ate breakfast.
At noon lei drank acqua e ran in yard.
In evening lei went a sleep early.
Che way sua corpo got rest, food, e cleanliness.

## 4. Risolviamo insieme

Quando è esso important a wash tuo hands?
Hands touch molti things.
Prima eating — so germs fare non get in tuo mouth.
Prima eating (e anche dopo using bathroom)
