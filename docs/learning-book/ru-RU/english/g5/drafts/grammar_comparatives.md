## 1. Что мы изучаем?

## 2. Простое объяснение

A short adjective: big becomes bigger than
A long adjective: we use more interesting than

This book is more interesting than that one.

He is taller than me.

## 3. Пример

Two books on the table.

## 4. Решим вместе
