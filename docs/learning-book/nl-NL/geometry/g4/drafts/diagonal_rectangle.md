## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag in meetkunde leren we over de diagonaal of een rechthoek.
We will see that in a 3 by 4 rechthoek — the diagonaal from corner to corner is 5.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: rectangle_diagonal
:::

Diagonaal of a rechthoek = a line from one corner to the opposite corner.
In a 3 by 4 rechthoek — the diagonaal forms a driehoek with zijden 3 and 4.
A familiar set of numbers: 3, 4, 5 — the diagonal is 5.
Here we learn this relationship — without requiring the vierkant root formula.

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Rechthoek:
length 4, width 3.
- Diagonaal from corner to corner: 5
- The triangle formed:
sides 3, 4, 5

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

For a 3 by 4 rectangle — wat is the length of the diagonal?
Identify sides 3 and 4.
In such een rechthoek de diagonaal = 5.

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

Rechthoek 6 by 8 — wat is the length of the diagonal?
Identify the sides:
6 and 8
These are multiples of the well-known 3-4-5 triangle:
3 × 2 = 6, and 4 × 2 = 8, so the diagonal:
5 × 2 = 10
The diagonal = 10
