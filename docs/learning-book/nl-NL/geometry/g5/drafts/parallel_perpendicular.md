## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we over parallel and perpendicular lines — with the notation: AB ∥ CD.
In a trapezoid, the bases AB and CD are parallel.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: parallel_lines
:::

Parallel lines — they never meet (in the same plane).
Notation: AB ∥ CD — line AB is parallel to line CD.
Perpendicular lines — 90° between them.
In trapezoid ABCD: AB and CD — the bases — are parallel.
In een rechthoek: AB ∥ CD, BC ∥ AD; AB ⊥ BC.

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Trapezoid ABCD:
- AB — top base
- CD — bottom base
- AB ∥ CD
Sides BC and AD — are not necessarily parallel to AB.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

In trapezoid ABCD — wat is the relationship between AB and CD?
AB and CD — the bases.
Bases → parallel: AB ∥ CD.
AB is parallel to CD

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

In rectangle ABCD — wat is the relationship between AB and BC?
In een rechthoek — all angles are 90°.
AB and BC are adjacent sides in een rechthoek — they meet at a 90° angle.
AB ⊥ BC (perpendicular)
