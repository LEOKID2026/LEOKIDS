## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we the height of a trapezoid from area and two bases.
Area = ((base1 + base2) × height) ÷ 2

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: trapezoid_height
:::

Isolate the height:
Height = (area × 2) ÷ (base1 + base2)

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Bases 5 cm and 9 cm, area 28 cm²:
5 + 9 = 14
Height = (28 × 2) ÷ 14 = 56 ÷ 14 = 4 cm

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

(28 × 2) ÷ 14 = 4
4 cm.

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

Trapezoid: bases 3 cm and 7 cm, area 30 cm² — wat is the height?
Add the bases:
3 + 7 = 10
Multiply the area by 2:
30 × 2 = 60
Divide by the sum of the bases:
60 ÷ 10 = 6
Height = 6 cm
