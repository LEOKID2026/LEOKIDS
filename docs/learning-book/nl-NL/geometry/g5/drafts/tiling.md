## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we over tiling a plane — covering the surface with tiles with no gaps and no overlaps.
Here — the angle of each tile matters.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Around every point where tiles meet — the sum of the angles = 360°.
Vierkant: angle 90° → 4 × 90° = 360°

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

4 squares at a corner: 4 × 90° = 360° — tiling is possible.
Equilateral triangle: 60° — 6 × 60° = 360°

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Tiling with squares — wat is the base angle of each tile?
Square → rechte hoek.
90°.

## 5. Probeer het zelf

Tiling with equilateral triangles — wat is the angle of each tile?
Hoeveel tiles meet at one point?
Try to solve by yourself.
Op de volgende pagina kijken we samen de stappen en het antwoord na.

---

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

Tiling with equilateral triangles — wat is the angle of each tile?
Hoeveel tiles meet at one point?
Angle in an equilateral triangle = 60°.
Sum of angles around a point = 360°.
360° ÷ 60° = 6
6 tiles meet at every point

## 7. Laten we oefenen!

Tiling depends on the hoeken of the tile.
In de oefening vind je tiling patterns — een vierkant's angle is 90°!
