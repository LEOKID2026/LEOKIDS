## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag in meetkunde leren we over parallel lines and perpendicular lines.
We will see an example from life: train tracks — parallel lines.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: parallel_lines
:::

Parallel — two lines going the same direction that never meet.
Example: twee rails van een treinspoor — evenwijdig.
Perpendicular — two lines that meet at a rechte hoek — 90°.
Example: the corner of a room — two walls.
Parallel ≠ perpendicular

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Treinsporen — twee rails naast elkaar:
- Same direction
- They do not meet
→ The lines are parallel.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Two rails of a train track — wat is the relationship between them?
Same direction, they do not meet.
→ Parallel.
Parallel

## 5. Probeer het zelf

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

Two walls in the corner of a room — they meet at a rechte hoek.
Parallel or perpendicular?
Check — do the lines meet? Yes, the walls meet at the corner.
They meet at a rechte hoek (90°) → perpendicular.
The walls are perpendicular

## 7. Laten we oefenen!
