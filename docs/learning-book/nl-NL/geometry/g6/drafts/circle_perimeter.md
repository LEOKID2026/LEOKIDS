## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we circumference of a circle in meetkunde — a new topic.
Circumference = 2 × π × radius.
In class: π ≈ 3.14

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: circle_perimeter
:::

The radius — the distance from the center of the circle to any point on the circle.
Formule:
Circumference = 2 × 3.14 × r
Unit: cm (length).

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Radius 5 cm:
2 × 3.14 × 5 = 6.28 × 5 = 31.4 cm

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Circle radius 5 cm (π = 3.14) — wat is de omtrek?
2 × 3.14 = 6.28
6.28 × 5 = 31.4
31.4 cm.
