## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we calculate volume of a balk directly — all three dimensions in one multiplication.
Volume = a × b × h — lengte × breedte × hoogte.
(Different from "prism": here it's a box — all vlakken are rechthoeks, without separating a "grondvlak" step.)

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

:::geometry-diagram
type: box_basic
:::

Balk — 3 dimensions: a, b, h.
Volume = a × b × h
Order of multiplication doesn't matter: 5 × 4 × 6 = 4 × 6 × 5 = 6 × 5 × 4
Unit: cm³.

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

Balk 5 cm × 4 cm × 6 cm:
- 5 × 4 = 20
- 20 × 6 = 120 cm³
Or: 5 × 4 × 6 = 120 — multiply all three together.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Balk 5 cm × 4 cm × 6 cm. Wat is het volume?
5 × 4 = 20
20 × 6 = 120
120 cm³.
