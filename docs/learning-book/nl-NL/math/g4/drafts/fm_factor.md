---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find the factors of a number.
A factor = a number that divides evenly with no remainder.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Factors of 24:
24 ÷ 1 = 24
24 ÷ 2 = 12
24 ÷ 3 = 8
24 ÷ 4 = 6
24 ÷ 6 = 4
24 ÷ 8 = 3
24 ÷ 12 = 2
24 ÷ 24 = 1
Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
Tip: look for multiplication pairs — 1×24, 2×12, 3×8, 4×6.
1 and the number itself — always factors.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Factors of 18
18 = 1×18, 2×9, 3×6
Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
