**Inhoudsbereik:** Addition with **two digits** after the decimal point (hundredths and tenths). Line up decimal points. Regrouping in tenths and hundredths. No three decimal digits.

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to add decimal numbers with two digits after the point — for example 3.45 + 2.30.
Important: line up the decimal points — hundredths under hundredths, tenths under tenths, whole numbers under whole numbers.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

3.45 + 2.30
3.45
+ 2.30
——
Hundredths: 5 + 0 = 5
Tenths: 4 + 3 = 7
Whole: 3 + 2 = 5
3.45 + 2.30 = 5.75
Rule: add column by column — like whole-number addition, just with the point in the same place.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

2.18 + 4.06
Hundredths: 8 + 6 = 14 → write 4, carry 1 to tenths
Tenths: 1 + 0 + 1 = 2
Whole: 2 + 4 = 6
2.18 + 4.06 = 6.24

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Work out: 1.35 + 3.42 = ?
Hundredths: 5 + 2 = 7
Tenths: 3 + 4 = 7
Whole: 1 + 3 = 4
1.35 + 3.42 = 4.77

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Work out: 4.63 + 1.25 = ?
Hundredths: 3 + 5 = 8
Tenths: 6 + 2 = 8
Whole: 4 + 1 = 5
4.63 + 1.25 = 5.88

## 7. Laten we oefenen!

Nu weet je hoe je add decimal numbers with two digits after the point.
Line up the points and work column by column!
