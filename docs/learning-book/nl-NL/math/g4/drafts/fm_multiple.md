## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we over multiples of a number.
A multiple = the result of multiplying the number by 1, 2, 3, 4…

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Multiples of 5:
5 × 1 = 5
5 × 2 = 10
5 × 3 = 15
5 × 4 = 20
5 × 5 = 25
5 × 6 = 30 …
Multiples of 5: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30…
Link to factors: if 5 is a factor of 20 — then 20 is a multiple of 5.
Tip: multiples of 5 end in 0 or 5 (like divisibility rules).

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Multiples of 4 (the first ones):
4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32…
4 × 1, 4 × 2, 4 × 3, 4 × 4, 4 × 5…

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Schrijf de eerste 5 veelvouden van 7.
7 × 1 = 7, 7 × 2 = 14, 7 × 3 = 21, 7 × 4 = 28, 7 × 5 = 35
7, 14, 21, 28, 35
