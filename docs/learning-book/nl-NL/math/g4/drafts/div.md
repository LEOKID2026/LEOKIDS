## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we strengthen division — equal sharing — with numbers up to about 1,000.
When there's no remainder, the division comes out evenly.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

480 ÷ 6 — 480 pencils in 6 equal boxes:
6 × 80 = 480 → 480 ÷ 6 = 80
720 ÷ 8: 8 × 90 = 720 → 720 ÷ 8 = 90
Tip: look in the times table — 6 × ? = 480
Check: divisor × quotient = dividend. Remainder — only on the division-with-remainder page.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

480 ÷ 6 — 480 pencils in 6 boxes:
480 ÷ 6 = 80
Check: 6 × 80 = 480
Box 1–6: 80 pencils in each
80 + 80 + 80 + 80 + 80 + 80 = 480

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Work out: 720 ÷ 8 = ?
Ask: 8 × ? = 720
8 × 90 = 720 (because 8 × 9 = 72, and 72 × 10 = 720).
Check: 8 × 90 = 720
720 ÷ 8 = 90

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Work out: 360 ÷ 9 = ?
Ask: 9 × ? = 360
9 × 4 = 36, so 9 × 40 = 360.
Check: 9 × 40 = 360
360 ÷ 9 = 40
