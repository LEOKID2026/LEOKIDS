## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we compare grote getallen up to 10,000 and pick the right symbol: > (greater than), < (less than), = (equal).

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Hoe vergelijken we grote getallen?
First thousands — who has more thousands?
If equal — hundreds
If equal — tens
If equal — ones
5,582 > 5,579 — thousands 5=5, hundreds 5=5, tens 8>7 → 5,582 is greater
4,560 and 4,650:
thousands 4=4, hundreds 5 < 6 → 4,560 < 4,650
The open side of the symbol points to the bigger number.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

8,934 ? 8,943
Thousands: 8 = 8
Hundreds: 9 = 9
Tens: 3 < 4
8,934 < 8,943
Symbol: 8,934 < 8,943

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Compare: 5,307 ? 5,370
Thousands: 5 = 5 — equal, keep going.
Hundreds: 3 = 3 — equal, keep going.
Tens: 0 < 7.
5,307 < 5,370.
5,307 < 5,370

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!
