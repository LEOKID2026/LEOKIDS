---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find the Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of two numbers.
It's the biggest factor they share.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Greatest Common Factor (GCF) of 18 and 24:
Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
Shared factors: 1, 2, 3, 6
Biggest: 6
GCF(18, 24) = 6
Tip: GCF helps simplify fractions and split into equal groups.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

GCF of 12 and 20
Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Factors of 20: 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20
Shared: 1, 2, 4 → GCF = 4

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Find the GCF of 15 and 25.
Factors of 15: 1, 3, 5, 15.
Factors of 25: 1, 5, 25.
Shared: 1, 5 → biggest: 5.
GCF(15, 25) = 5
