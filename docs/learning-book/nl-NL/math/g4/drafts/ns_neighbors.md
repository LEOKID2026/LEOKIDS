## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we find neighbors in grote getallen — up to 10,000.
±1 even when crossing a thousand — for example 1,999 → 2,000 → 2,001.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Neighbor before = −1
Neighbor after = +1
2,450:
- before: 2,449
- after: 2,451
Crossing a thousand:
- 1,999 → after = 2,000
- 2,000 → before = 1,999
Place value: when you cross a thousand, all the digits can change — but the jump is still 1.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

3,628:
3,628 − 1 = 3,627
3,628 + 1 = 3,629
2,999 → 3,000: the neighbor after 2,999 = 3,000 — crossing a thousand, still just +1.
3,626 — 3,627 — [3,628] — 3,629 — 3,630
