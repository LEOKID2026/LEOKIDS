---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

A pair that makes 10: two numbers that add to 10.
8 + 2 = 10 | 7 + 3 = 10 | 6 + 4 = 10 | 5 + 5 = 10
Strategy — "make 10" in a big addition:
28 + 15 = ?
1. 28 + 2 = 30 (the pair 8+2 in the last digits)
2.
13 left from 15 → 2 + 13 = 15
30 + 13 = 43
48 + 27 = ?
- 48 + 2 = 50, 25 left → 50 + 25 = 75
No tientalraam needed — just pairs from memory.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

38 + 9 = ?
8 + 2 = 10 → 38 + 2 = 40
7 left from 9 → 40 + 7 = 47
38 + 9 = 47

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

8 + ? = 10 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
You have 8. You want 10.
8 + 2 = 10 — the pair 8 and 2.
The ontbrekende getal is 2.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

6 + ? = 10 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
You have 6. You want to reach 10.
10 − 6 = 4
Check: 6 + 4 = 10
The ontbrekende getal is 4
