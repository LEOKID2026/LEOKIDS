## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we about number neighbors.
Every number has a number that comes before it and a number that comes after it — like neighbors who live right next door.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Neighbor before = the number that comes right before (one step left op de getallenlijn).
Neighbor after = the number that comes right after (one step right op de getallenlijn).
Op de getallenlijn: left = one less, right = one more.
For the neighbors of 5:
- Neighbor before: 4
- Neighbor after: 6

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Wat zijn de buren van 8?
Find 8 op de getallenlijn.
One step to the left → 7 (neighbor before)
One step to the right → 9 (neighbor after)
The neighbors of 8 are 7 and 9.
