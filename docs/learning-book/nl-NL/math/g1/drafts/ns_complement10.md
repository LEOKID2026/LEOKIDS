## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we about pairs that make 10 — two numbers that together give 10.
When you know these pairs, adding and subtracting get easier!

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Er zijn speciale paren die altijd samen 10 maken:
1 + 9, 2 + 8, 3 + 7, 4 + 6, 5 + 5
Important: order doesn't matter!
3 + 7 = 10 and also 7 + 3 = 10

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Ten-frame — 3 spots filled, 7 spots empty:
Label: 3 + 7 = 10. The partner of 3 is 7.
Example with apples:
I have 6 apples.
Hoeveel more do I need to have 10? 4 — because 6 + 4 = 10.

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

In the tientalraam — fill 8 spots
2 spots are left empty
The partner of 8 is 2 — because 8 + 2 = 10
8 + 2 = 10
