## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we percent discount.
Price 180 sjekel, 20% off.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

20% of 180 = 180 × 20 ÷ 100 = 36 sjekel discount.
Final price = 180 − 36 = 144 sjekel

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

15% of 200 = 30 → 200 − 30 = 170

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Price 180, 20% off. Final price?
Discount = 180 × 20 ÷ 100 = 36.
180 − 36 = 144
144 sjekel.
