---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we: a fraction = division.
3/4 means 3 ÷ 4.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

a/b = a ÷ b
3/4: 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75
5/8: 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

1/2 = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Wat is the value of 3/4 as a decimal?
3 ÷ 4 = 0.75
0.75 (or 3/4).

---

## 5. Probeer het zelf

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Calculate: 5/8 as a decimal
Fraction = division → 5 ÷ 8.
5 ÷ 8 = 0.625
Check: 0.625 × 8 = 5
The answer: 0.625

---

## 7. Laten we oefenen!

The fraction-as-division connection is the foundation for multiplying and dividing fractions.
In de oefening vind je fractions as division — numerator divided by denominator!
