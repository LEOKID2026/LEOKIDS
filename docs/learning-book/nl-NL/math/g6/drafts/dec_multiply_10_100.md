**Inhoudsbereik:** moving the decimal point to the right

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we multiply a decimal by 10 or 100.
3.25 × 10 — the decimal point moves to the right.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

3.25 × 10 = 32.5 — the decimal point moves one place to the right.
3.25 × 100 = 325 — two places to the right.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

0.45 × 10 = 4.5

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

3.25 × 10 = ?
×10 → the decimal point moves one place to the right.
32.5.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

×10 → the decimal point moves one place to the right.
4.17 → 41.7

The answer: 41.7
