## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve problems about distance, time, and speed.
Distance = speed × time

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

A runner goes 60 km/h for 2 hours.
Distance = 60 × 2 = 120 km

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Speed = distance ÷ time.
Time = distance ÷ speed.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

60 km/h, 2 hours. Distance?
60 × 2 = 120
120 km.
