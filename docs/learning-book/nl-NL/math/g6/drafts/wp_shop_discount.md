## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve store sale problems.
A shirt costs 150 sjekel, 15% off.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

15% of 150 = 150 × 15 ÷ 100 = 22.50 sjekel.
150 − 22.50 = 127.50 sjekel
