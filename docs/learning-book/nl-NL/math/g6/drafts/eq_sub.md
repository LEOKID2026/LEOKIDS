## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve subtraction equations — a ontbrekende getal with large numbers.
5,000 − ? = 3,725

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

200,000 − ? = 175,000 — ontbrekende getal in the middle:
? = minuend − result = 200,000 − 175,000
Rule: when __ comes after the minus sign — subtract the result from the minuend.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

350,000 − __ = 218,000
__ = 350,000 − 218,000 = 132,000
Check: 350,000 − 132,000 = 218,000

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

200,000 − ? = 175,000
? = 200,000 − 175,000 = 25,000
Check: 200,000 − 25,000 = 175,000
25,000.
