**Inhoudsbereik:** greatest common factor (GCF) of two numbers

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we the greatest common factor (GCF) — the largest factor that two numbers share.
30 and 45.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Factors of 30: 1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30
Factors of 45: 1,3,5,9,15,45
Shared: 1,3,5,15 → GCF = 15

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

18 and 24: shared factors 1,2,3,6 → GCF = 6

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

GCF of 30 and 45?
Shared factors: 1, 3, 5, 15.
The largest = 15.
15.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

GCF of 24 and 36
Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36.
Shared factors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 → the largest = 12.
The answer: GCF = 12
