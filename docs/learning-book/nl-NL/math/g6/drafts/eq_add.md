## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve addition equations — a ontbrekende getal with large numbers.
2,450 + ? = 4,030

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

150,000 + ? = 400,000 — ontbrekende getal in addition:
? = sum − known number = 400,000 − 150,000
Rule: missing = sum − known — not adding the two numbers you see!

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

250,000 + __ = 500,000
__ = 500,000 − 250,000 = 250,000
Check: 250,000 + 250,000 = 500,000

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

150,000 + ? = 400,000
? = 400,000 − 150,000 = 250,000
Check: 150,000 + 250,000 = 400,000
250,000.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Solve: 2,450 + ? = 4,030
? = sum − known number = 4,030 − 2,450 = 1,580.
Check: 2,450 + 1,580 = 4,030
The answer: ? = 1,580
