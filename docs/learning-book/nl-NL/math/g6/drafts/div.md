## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we strengthen division — equal sharing with no remainder — using multi-step breaking apart and checking.
We ask: hoeveel times does the divisor fit into the dividend? And we check each step.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

144,000 ÷ 12 — break apart in steps (large numbers):
Step A: 12 × 10,000 = 120,000 → remainder 24,000
Step B: 12 × 2,000 = 24,000 → remainder 0
Summary: 10,000 + 2,000 = 12,000
144,000 ÷ 12 = 12,000
Check: 12,000 × 12 = 144,000

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

360,000 ÷ 15 — break apart:
15 × 20,000 = 300,000 → remainder 60,000
15 × 4,000 = 60,000 → remainder 0
20,000 + 4,000 = 24,000
360,000 ÷ 15 = 24,000
Check: 24,000 × 15 = 360,000

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 96,000 ÷ 8 = ?
- 8 × 10,000 = 80,000
- Remainder: 96,000 − 80,000 = 16,000
8 × 2,000 = 16,000 remainder: 0.
Step 3 — check: (10,000 + 2,000) × 8 = 12,000 × 8 = 96,000
12,000

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Calculate: 1,440 ÷ 12
12 × 100 = 1,200 → remainder 1,440 − 1,200 = 240.
12 × 20 = 240 → remainder 0.
100 + 20 = 120
Check: 120 × 12 = 1,440
The answer: 120
