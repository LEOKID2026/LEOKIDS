**Inhoudsbereik:** difference between two large quantities

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve a question: hoeveel more? (the difference)
Identify two quantities — look for the difference, not the total!

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

"Hoeveel more?" = the difference — not the total!
City A: 250,000 people. City B: 198,000 people.
Hoeveel more in A? → 250,000 − 198,000 (larger − smaller).
Do not add — subtract!

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Factory A: 180,000 units. Factory B: 142,500 units.
180,000 − 142,500 = 37,500 more units at A.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

250,000 people in City A, 198,000 in City B. Hoeveel more in A?
250,000 > 198,000.
250,000 − 198,000 = 52,000
52,000 more people.

---

## 5. Probeer het zelf

A has 12,450 books, B has 9,875.
Hoeveel more at A?
Probeer het zelf op te lossen.
Op de volgende pagina kijken we samen de stappen en het antwoord na.

---

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

A has 12,450 books, B has 9,875 — hoeveel more at A?
"Hoeveel more?" = difference → subtraction.
12,450 − 9,875 = 2,575
Check: 9,875 + 2,575 = 12,450
The answer: 2,575 more books

---

## 7. Laten we oefenen!

Difference questions appear often — with large numbers.
In de oefening vind je "hoeveel more?" — subtract the smaller from the larger!
