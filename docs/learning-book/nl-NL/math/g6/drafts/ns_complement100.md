## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we strengthen pairs that make 100 — and see how they connect to decimals and percents.
When two parts together make 100% (or 1 whole) — it is the same idea as 63 + 37 = 100.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

63 + 37 = 100 — a bekende paar.
Connection to decimals:
- 0.63 + 0.37 = 1.00 (one whole)
- 0.63 = 63 hundredths → 63%
- 0.37 = 37 hundredths → 37%
- 63% + 37% = 100%
More pairs:
- 82 + 18 = 100 → 0.82 + 0.18 = 1.00
- 45 + 55 = 100 → 45% + 55% = 100%
The idea: two parts that complete a whole — whether 100, 1.00, or 100%.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

18 + ? = 100
100 − 18 = 82
Check: 18 + 82 = 100
Connection: 18% + 82% = 100%
0.18 + 0.82 = 1.00

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

63 + ? = 100 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
100 − 63 = 37
63 + 37 = 100
Step 3 (connection): 63% + 37% = 100%
The ontbrekende getal is 37.
