## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we find number neighbors — ±1 — even when we cross a hundred boundary.
Bijvoorbeeld: 399 → 400 → 401 — each step is only 1.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Neighbor before = −1 (one step back).
Neighbor after = +1 (one step forward).
350:
- before: 349
- after: 351
Crossing a hundred:
- 499 → after = 500
- 500 → before = 499
999 → after = 1,000 (we cross into a new hundred!)

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

628 op de getallenlijn:
628 − 1 = 627
628 + 1 = 629
399 → 400: the neighbor after 399 = 400 — a hundred boundary, but still +1.
626 — 627 — [628] — 629 — 630
