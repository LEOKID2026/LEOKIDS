## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we multiply by hundreds — for example 5 × 200.
It's like multiplying by tens — but with two zeros at the end.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

5 × 200:
5 × 2 = 10
Add 00 (hundreds): 1000
5 × 200 = 1000
3 × 400: 3 × 4 = 12 → 1200
Rule: multiply the digit (without the zeros), then put two zeros at the end.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

6 × 300
6 × 3 = 18
6 × 300 = 1800

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 4 × 500 = ?

4 × 500 = 2000
