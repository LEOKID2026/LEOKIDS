---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we division with a remainder — when it doesn't divide evenly, something is left over.
Er is a quotient (hoeveel in each group) and a remainder (what's left).

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

23 ÷ 5 — 23 cookies, 5 in each box:
5 × 4 = 20 — 4 in each box (4 full boxes).
23 − 20 = 3 — 3 cookies left over.
23 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 3
Check: 5 × 4 + 3 = 23 — the remainder is always smaller than the divisor (3 < 5).

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

17 ÷ 4 — 17 cookies in 4 boxes:
4 × 4 = 16 — 4 in each box
17 − 16 = 1 — 1 cookie left over
17 ÷ 4 = 4 remainder 1

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 29 ÷ 6 = ? (with remainder)
6 × 4 = 24
29 − 24 = 5
Check: 6 × 4 + 5 = 29
4 remainder 5

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Calculate: 19 ÷ 3
3 × 6 = 18 — 6 full groups.
19 − 18 = 1 — 1 left over.
Check: 3 × 6 + 1 = 19
19 ÷ 3 = 6 remainder 1
