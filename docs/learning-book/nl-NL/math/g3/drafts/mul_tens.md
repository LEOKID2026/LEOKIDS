## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we multiply by tens — for example 4 × 30.
It's like multiplying by 4, then adding a 0 (moving to the tens place).

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

4 × 30:
Think: 4 × 3 = 12
Add a 0 (because 30 = 3 tens): 120
4 × 30 = 120
Rule: multiply the digit (without the 0), then put a 0 at the end.
6 × 5 = 30, so 6 × 50 = 300

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

7 × 40
7 × 4 = 28
7 × 40 = 280

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen
