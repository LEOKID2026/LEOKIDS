## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we spot even and odd numbers — even when the number is big (up to 1,000).
Ask: can we split into 2 equal groups with nothing left over?

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

An even number — you can split it into 2 equal groups (with no remainder).
Bijvoorbeeld: 248 = 124 + 124 → even.
An odd number — when you split into 2, 1 is left over.
Bijvoorbeeld: 351 = 175 + 175 + 1 → odd.
Quick shortcut: look only at the ones digit (the rightmost digit):
- 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 → even
- 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 → odd
It doesn't matter hoeveel hundreds or tens — only the ones digit decides.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

712 — even or odd?
Way 1 (splitting): 712 ÷ 2 = 356 — no remainder → even.
Way 2 (ones digit): the ones digit is 2 → even.
356 + 356 = 712

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Is 905 even or odd?
Ones digit: 5.
5 is odd (1, 3, 5, 7, 9).
When divided by 2, 1 will be left over → 905 is odd.
905 is odd.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Is 48 even or odd?
The ones digit of 48 is 8.
8 belongs to 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 — even
Check: 48 ÷ 2 = 24 — no remainder.
48 is even
