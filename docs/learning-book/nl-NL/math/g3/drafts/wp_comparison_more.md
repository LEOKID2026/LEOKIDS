## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve a word problem — when the question asks hoeveel more one person has than another.
We need to find the difference — bigger minus smaller.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Framework:
Wat weten we? Two amounts
Wat wordt gevraagd? Hoeveel more for the one who has more
Wat doen we? Aftrekken: groter − kleiner
"More" = difference — not a total!

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Noa heeft 52 cards and Yuval has 37 cards.
Hoeveel more cards does Noa have than Yuval?
We know: 52, 37
We're asked:
The difference (more)
We do: 52 − 37 = 15
15 more cards.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Dana has 68 stickers and Roni has 45 stickers. Hoeveel more stickers does Dana have?
Dana has more — 68 > 45.
68 − 45 = 23
23 more stickers.

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Gal has 75 and Ella has 48 — hoeveel more does Gal have?
Gal has more: 75 > 48.
75 − 48 = 27
Check: 48 + 27 = 75
Gal has 27 more stickers
