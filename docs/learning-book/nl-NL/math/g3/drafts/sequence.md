## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we number sequences — a list of numbers that go up (or down) by one steady rule.
First we find the step, then we continue the sequence — even with numbers up to 1,000.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

A sequence = numbers in a row following one rule.
A fixed step: each time we add (or subtract) the same number.
5, 10, 15, 20, …
- 10 − 5 = 5
- 15 − 10 = 5
- The step: +5 each time
The next number: 20 + 5 = 25
Op de getallenlijn: each step = a jump of the same size.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

235, 240, 245, 250, ?
240 − 235 = 5
245 − 240 = 5
250 − 245 = 5
Step: +5
250 + 5 = 255
The answer: 255

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Continue: 180, 185, 190, 195, ?
185 − 180 = 5 The step is +5.
195 + 5 = 200
200
