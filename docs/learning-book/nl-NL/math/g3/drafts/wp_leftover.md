---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve: hoeveel are left over? — when we split things into equal groups and not everything fits.
The answer is the remainder — what's left outside the full groups.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Imagine 47 children going on a trip — split into groups of 6.
Hoeveel full groups? 6 × 7 = 42 (7 groups).
What's left over? 47 − 42 = 5 — 5 children without a full group.
5 = the remainder — that's the answer to "hoeveel are left over?", not 7 (the number of groups).

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

31 balloons — split into pairs (2 in each group).
2 × 15 = 30 — 15 full pairs.
31 − 30 = 1 — 1 balloon left over (remainder).
1 balloon (remainder).

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Er zijn 43 stickers. You put 5 on each page. Hoeveel stickers will be left without a full page?
5 × 8 = 40 (8 full pages).
43 − 40 = 3 — 3 stickers left over (remainder).
3 stickers (remainder).

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

38 children in groups of 5 — hoeveel are left over?
5 × 7 = 35 — 7 full groups.
38 − 35 = 3 — 3 children left without a full group.
Check: 5 × 7 + 3 = 38
3 children left over
