## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we review pairs that make 10 — a foundation for fast addition and making 100.
Know that 7 + 3 = 10?
The same idea works for 37 + 63 = 100 too.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

A pair for 10: two numbers that add up to 10.
All the pairs:
1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5
Using it in addition: "make 10 first" — then keep going.
Example: 9 + 6
- 9 + 1 = 10, 5 left over
10 + 5 = 15
Link to Groep 5: 7 + 3 = 10 → 67 + 33 = 100 (the same idea with tens).

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

8 + 5 = ?
8 needs 2 to reach 10 → 8 + 2 = 10
3 left from 5 → 10 + 3 = 13
8 + 5 = 13
The pair: 8 and 2 make 10.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

6 + ? = 10 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
You have 6. You want to reach 10.
6 + 4 = 10 — the pair is 6 and 4.
The ontbrekende getal is 4.
