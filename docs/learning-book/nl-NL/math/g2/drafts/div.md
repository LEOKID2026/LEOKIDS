**Inhoudsbereik:** Division as fair sharing. Connection to multiplication. Small numbers (up to ~20). No remainder, no long division, no formal algorithm.

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we division — sharing equally among everyone.
When you divide fairly, each person gets the same amount.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Division = split the whole amount into equal groups (or give each person the same share).
12 ÷ 3 = 4 means:
- Er zijn 12 objects
- Split into 3 equal groups
- Each group has 4
Connection to multiplication: 3 × 4 = 12, so 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
Important: here we don't practice remainders or long division — only simple fair sharing.

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 12 ÷ 3 = ?
Er zijn 12 objects, split into 3 equal groups.
Split: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 — 3 groups of 4.
Check: 3 × 4 = 12
12 ÷ 3 = 4
