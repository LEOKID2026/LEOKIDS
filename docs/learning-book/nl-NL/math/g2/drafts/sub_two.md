## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we leren om te subtract two numbers up to 100 — in a row, in your head, or in writing.
We'll break each number into tens and ones to make it easier.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

When subtracting two numbers, you can think like this:
1. Subtract tens from tens
2. Subtract ones from ones
3. Add the results together
Example: 68 − 24
- Tens: 60 − 20 = 40
- Ones: 8 − 4 = 4
- Total: 40 + 4 = 44
Important: on this page we subtract in a row. Vertical subtraction is learned on a separate page.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

68 − 24 — break into tens and ones:
68 − 24 = 44
You can also think: 68 − 20 = 48, then 48 − 4 = 44.
60 + 8 = 68
20 + 4 = 24
Tens: 60 − 20 = 40
Ones: 8 − 4 = 4
40 + 4 = 44

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 68 − 24 = ?
Break apart — 60 + 8 = 68, and 20 + 4 = 24.
Subtract tens: 60 − 20 = 40
Subtract ones: 8 − 4 = 4
40 + 4 = 44
68 − 24 = 44

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Calculate: 75 − 33 = ?
Step 1 — tens: 70 − 30 = 40
Step 2 — ones: 5 − 3 = 2
Step 3 — add: 40 + 2 = 42
75 − 33 = 42
