## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we leren om te add two numbers up to 100 — in a row, in your head, or in writing.
We'll break each number into tens and ones to make it easier.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

When adding two numbers, you can think like this:
1.
Add the tens to the tens
2.
Add the ones to the ones
3.
Add the two results together
Example: 34 + 25
- Tens: 30 + 20 = 50
- Ones: 4 + 5 = 9
- Total: 50 + 9 = 59
Important: on this page we add in a row. Vertical addition is learned on a separate page.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

58 + 37 — breaking into tens and ones:
58 + 37 = 95
50 + 8 = 58
30 + 7 = 37
Tens: 50 + 30 = 80
Ones: 8 + 7 = 15 → 5, carry 1
80 + 15 = 95

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Calculate: 47 + 45 = ?
Tens: 40 + 40 = 80
Ones: 7 + 5 = 12 → 2, carry 1.
80 + 12 = 92
47 + 45 = 92
