## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag herhalen we paren die samen 10 maken — twee getallen die samen 10 geven.
Know the pairs? Adding in your head gets easier!

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Pairs for 10:
1 + 9, 2 + 8, 3 + 7, 4 + 6, 5 + 5
Important: the order doesn't matter!
7 + 3 = 10 and also 3 + 7 = 10
How does this help with addition?
In 8 + 5 you can think: 8 needs 2 to reach 10, and then 3 is left.
8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

7 + ? = 10 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
In the tientalraam — 7 spots are filled.
3 spots are left empty.
The partner of 7 is 3 — because 7 + 3 = 10.
7 + 3 = 10

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

The partner of 6 is 4 — because 6 + 4 = 10.
6 + 4 = 10
