## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag gaan we word problems about days of the week.
Today, tomorrow, in hoeveel days — hop along the row of days.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Row of days of the week:
In one day = one hop forward.
In 2 days = two hops forward.
Before you answer:
1. Wat weten we? — welke dag is het vandaag
2. Wat wordt gevraagd? — which day it will be (after hops)
3. Wat doen we? — tel sprongen op de rij
Important: days of the week only — no clock, no dates in the month.
[Sunday] [Monday] [Tuesday] [Wednesday] [Thursday] [Friday] [Saturday]

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Vandaag is het maandag.
In 3 days — which day?
3 hops → Thursday.
[●Monday] → [Tuesday] → [Wednesday] → [●Thursday]

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Vandaag is het maandag. Over 3 dagen — welke dag is het?
Stap 1 — Wat weten we?
Today: Monday.
Stap 2 — Wat wordt gevraagd?
Which day in 3 days.
Stap 3 — Wat doen we?
Monday → (1) Tuesday → (2) Wednesday → (3) Thursday
Thursday
