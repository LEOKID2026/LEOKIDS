**Inhoudsbereik:** Quotient and remainder; large numbers (1,247÷8). Remainder check: divisor × quotient + remainder = dividend.

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we division with a remainder — when the division doesn't come out exactly.
You get a quotient and a remainder, and you check with the remainder check.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

1,247 ÷ 8:
8 × 155 = 1,240
1,247 − 1,240 = 7
1,247 ÷ 8 = 155 remainder 7
Remainder check: 8 × 155 + 7 = 1,247
The remainder is always less than the divisor (7 < 8).

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

523 ÷ 6:
6 × 87 = 522
523 − 522 = 1
523 ÷ 6 = 87 remainder 1
Check: 6 × 87 + 1 = 523

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Work out: 1,247 ÷ 8 = ? (with remainder)
8 × 155 = 1,240
1,247 − 1,240 = 7
Step 3 — remainder check: 8 × 155 + 7 = 1,247
155 remainder 7
