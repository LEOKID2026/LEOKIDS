## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we see how pairs that make 100 also help with estimation — when you don't need an exact answer, just one that's close.
The pair 37 + 63 = 100 is useful even when you round numbers.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

37 + 63 = 100 — a pair worth remembering.
Link to estimation:
- 37 is close to 40 (round to the nearest ten)
- 63 is close to 60
- 40 + 60 = 100 — a quick estimate!
Estimating addition: 37 + 58 ≈ ?
- 37 ≈ 40, 58 ≈ 60
- 40 + 60 = 100 — the real answer (95) is close to 100
Paren die 100 maken = een basis om te denken "hoeveel ontbreekt om 100 te bereiken" — ook bij schatten.

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Schatting: ongeveer hoeveel is 52 + 49?
52 is close to 50. 49 is close to 50.
50 + 50 = 100 — estimate: about 100.
Check: 52 + 49 = 101 — very close!
52 + 48 = 100 exactly — 52 and 48 are a pair that make 100.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

37 + ? = 100 — wat is het ontbrekende getal?
100 − 37 = 63
Check: 30 + 70 = 100, and 7 + 3 = 10 → 100
Step 3 (estimation): 37 ≈ 40, 63 ≈ 60 → 40 + 60 = 100 — the pair fits.
The ontbrekende getal is 63.
