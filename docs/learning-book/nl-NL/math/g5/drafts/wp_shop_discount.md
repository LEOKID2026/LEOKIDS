**Inhoudsbereik:** Price after percent discount

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve a shopping question with a percent discount.
First find the discount, then subtract from the price.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Price $200, discount 15%:
15% of 200 = 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30
200 − 30 = $170
Price after discount: $170

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

$350, discount 10%:
10% = 35; 350 − 35 = $315

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

An item costs $200, discount 15%. Wat is the price?
15% of 200 = 30
200 − 30 = 170
$170

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

An item $300, discount 20% — wat is the price after the discount?
20% of 300 = 300 × 20 ÷ 100 = 60
300 − 60 = 240
The price after the discount: $240
