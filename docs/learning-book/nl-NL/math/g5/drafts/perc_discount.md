**Inhoudsbereik:** Price after discount

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find the price after a percent discount.
A discount = you take away a percent of the price.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Price $150, discount 20%:
20% of 150 = 150 × 20 ÷ 100 = 30
150 − 30 = 120
Price after discount: $120
(or: 80% of 150 = 120)

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

$200, discount 10%:
10% of 200 = 20
200 − 20 = $180

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

A shirt costs $150, discount 20%. Wat is the price?
20% of 150 = 30
150 − 30 = 120
$120

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Prijs $240, korting 25% — wat is de prijs na de korting?
25% of 240 = 240 × 25 ÷ 100 = 60
240 − 60 = 180
The price after the discount: $180
