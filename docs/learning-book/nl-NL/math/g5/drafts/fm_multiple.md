## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find multiples of a number — numbers that the number divides evenly.
Multiples = results of multiplying by the number.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Multiples of 7:
7 × 1 = 7
7 × 2 = 14
7 × 3 = 21
7 × 4 = 28
7 × 5 = 35
In our practice, when we ask for the first multiples, we usually start with the positive ones: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35…

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60…

---
