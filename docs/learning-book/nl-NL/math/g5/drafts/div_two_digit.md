## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to divide by a two-digit number — the long division algorithm.
This is an important skill for solving problems.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

2,450 ÷ 35 — two-digit divisor:
Estimate — 35 × 70 = 2,450
2,450 ÷ 35 = 70
Long algorithm: start from the highest digits, estimate hoeveel times 35 fits, bring down and continue.
Check: 70 × 35 = 2,450

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

3,675 ÷ 49:
49 × 75 = 3,675 → 3,675 ÷ 49 = 75
Check: 75 × 49 = 3,675

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Work out 2,450 ÷ 35 = ?
35 × 70 = 2,450
2,450 ÷ 35 = 70
70
