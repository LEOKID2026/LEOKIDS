**Inhoudsbereik:** Estimating quantity without exact counting

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to estimate a quantity — without counting exactly.
Estimating quantity helps in everyday life and bij rekenen.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

About hoeveel are 12,000 leaves?
You don't count one by one — you estimate:
- A small group ≈ 100
- Hoeveel groups like that? ≈ 120 groups
- 120 × 100 ≈ 12,000
Estimate:
About 12,000

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

About hoeveel are 500 marbles in a box?
Look at one layer — ≈ 50, and 10 layers → ≈ 500

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

A hall has about 12,000 seats. Is 8,000 seats "about" right?
8,000 is far from 12,000
10,000 or 12,000 — closer
12,000 — a reasonable estimate; 8,000 — too far off

---

## 5. Probeer het zelf

A hall has about 9,000 chairs.
Is 8,800 a reasonable estimate?
Probeer het zelf op te lossen.

---

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

A hall has about 9,000 chairs.
Is 8,800 a reasonable estimate?
Controleer de ordegrootte: 8.800 ligt dicht bij 9.000
The difference: 9,000 − 8,800 = 200 — a relatively small gap
8,800 is a reasonable estimate — same order of magnitude as 9,000
Yes, 8,800 is a reasonable estimate
