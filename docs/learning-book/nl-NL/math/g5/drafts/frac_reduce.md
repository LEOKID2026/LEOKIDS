## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to simplify a fraction — write it in its simplest form.
Simplifying helps you compare and add fractions.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Simplifying = divide the numerator and denominator by the same number (GCF).
12/18:
GCF of 12 and 18 = 6
12 ÷ 6 = 2, 18 ÷ 6 = 3
12/18 = 2/3

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

15/25:
GCF(15, 25) = 5
15/25 = 3/5

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Simplify 12/18
