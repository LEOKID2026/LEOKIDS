## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find all the factors of a number — bij rekenen and number theory.
A factor = a number that divides the number exactly with no remainder.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Factors of 36:
1 × 36 = 36
2 × 18 = 36
3 × 12 = 36
4 × 9 = 36
6 × 6 = 36
Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36
