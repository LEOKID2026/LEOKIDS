## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to solve a multiplication equation — find the missing factor.
__ × 12 = 480

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

__ × 12 = 480
__ = 480 ÷ 12 = 40
Check: 40 × 12 = 480
Rule:
Missing factor = product ÷ known factor

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

7 × __ = 350
__ = 350 ÷ 7 = 50

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

__ × 12 = 480 — wat is missing?

__ = 40
