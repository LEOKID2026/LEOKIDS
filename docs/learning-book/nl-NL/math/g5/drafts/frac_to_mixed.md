## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to change an improper fraction to a mixed number.
An improper fraction = numerator is greater than or equal to the denominator.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

7/3 → mixed number:
Divide: 7 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 1
Whole = 2, fraction = 1/3
7/3 = 2 1/3

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

11/4:
11 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 3
11/4 = 2 3/4

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Change 7/3 to a mixed number
7 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 1
2 1/3
