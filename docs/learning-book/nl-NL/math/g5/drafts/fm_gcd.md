**Inhoudsbereik:** Greatest common factor (GCF) of two numbers

---

## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to find the greatest common factor (GCF) of two numbers.
It's the largest factor they share.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

GCF of 30 and 45:
Factors of 30: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30
Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45
Common factors: 1, 3, 5, 15
Largest: 15
GCF(30, 45) = 15

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

The GCF of 24 and 36 is 12.
