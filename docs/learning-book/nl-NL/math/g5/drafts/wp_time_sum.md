## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we solve a word problem about adding times — hours and minutes together.
When the question says "total" or "in totaal" — you add, you don't subtract!

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Here we write time as hours:minutes (for example 1:25 = 1 hour and 25 minutes).
How to add:
1. Add the minutes — if you get 60 or more, change to an hour (60 min = 1:00).
2. Add the hours — including any hour from the minutes.
0:55 + 1:20:
- Minutes: 55 + 20 = 75 → 1:15
- Hours: 0 + 1 + 1 = 2 → 2:15

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

A hike: drive 0:40, walk 1:15.
Minutes: 40 + 15 = 55 → 0:55 (less than 60 — no change).
Hours: 0 + 1 = 1
1:55.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Music lesson 1:25 + practice 0:45. Wat is the total activity time?
Step 1 — minutes: 25 + 45 = 70 → 1:10 (70 min = 1 hour and 10).
Step 2 — hours: 1 + 0 + 1 = 2
2:10.
