## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we to expand a fraction — multiply the numerator and denominator by the same number.
Expanding helps you get a common denominator before adding.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Expanding = multiply the numerator and denominator by the same number
2/3 with denominator 12:
12 ÷ 3 = 4 → multiply by 4
2/3 = 8/12
Check: 2/3 = 8/12  (same value)

---

## 3. Visueel / concreet voorbeeld

3/5 = ?/20
20 ÷ 5 = 4 → 3×4=12
3/5 = 12/20

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

Expand 2/3 to have denominator 12
12 ÷ 3 = 4
2 × 4 = 8
8/12

## 6. Veelgemaakte fout — let op!

Expand 3/4 to have denominator 20.
20 ÷ 4 = 5 — that's the number you multiply by
Numerator: 3 × 5 = 15
Denominator: 4 × 5 = 20
3/4 = 15/20
