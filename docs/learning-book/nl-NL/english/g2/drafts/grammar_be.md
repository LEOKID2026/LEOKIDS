## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we strengthen the patterns I am, You are — and add He is and She is.
We already learned I am — now let's add more pronouns.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Patterns:
I am — I
You are — you
He is — he
She is — she
Voorbeeld:
She is happy.
She is happy.

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

A happy girl.
She is happy.
She is happy.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

How do we say "she is happy"?
She is happy

---

## 5. Probeer het zelf

A sad boy.
He is sad
Probeer het zelf op te lossen.
Op de volgende pagina kijken we samen de manier en het antwoord na.

---

## 6. Laten we samen nakijken

The question: a sad boy — in English?
He means he.
sad means sad.
The answer is: He is sad.

## 7. Laten we oefenen!
