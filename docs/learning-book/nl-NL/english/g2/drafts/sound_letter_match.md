## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag zullen we strengthen the connection between sound and letter.
When we hear an "m" sound at the start of a word, we Let op the matching letter.
When we Kijk naar a letter, we think about what sound it can make in a word.

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

We hear an "m" sound at the start of a word — we look for the letter M.
We hear an "s" sound at the start of a word — we look for the letter S.
