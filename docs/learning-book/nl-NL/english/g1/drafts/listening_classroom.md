## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we listen to short instructions in English in the classroom.
One step at a time — first we listen.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

Stand up. — Get up.
Sit down. — Sit down.
Open your book. — Open your book.
Close your book. — Close your book.

---

## 3. Voorbeeld

The teacher says: "Stand up."
We stand up — that's the instruction.
The teacher says: "Sit down."
We sit down.

---

## 4. Laten we het samen oplossen

What do we do when we hear "Open your book"?
Open means to open.
Book means a book.
So we open our book.
Open your book means: open the book.

---

## 5. Probeer het zelf

What do we do when we hear "Close your book"?
Probeer het zelf op te lossen.
Op de volgende pagina kijken we samen de manier en het antwoord na.

Close means to close.
Book means a book.
So we close the book.
Close your book means: close the book.
