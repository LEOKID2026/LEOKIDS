## 1. Wat gaan we leren?

Vandaag leren we connect words into a short sentence in English.
A short sentence — 2 or 3 words.

---

## 2. Eenvoudige uitleg

We take words we've learned — and connect them.
I am happy — I am happy.
I am sad — I am sad.
You are happy — You are happy.

## 3. Voorbeeld
