## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos la diferencia entre Simple y Continuous.
I read every day comparado con I am reading now.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Simple — un hábito:
He walks every day.

Continuous — ocurriendo ahora:
She is reading now.

---

## 3. Example

Una niña leyendo un libro en este momento.

She is reading now.
