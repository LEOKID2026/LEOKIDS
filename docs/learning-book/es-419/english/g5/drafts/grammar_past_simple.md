## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos Past Simple.
I played football yesterday.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Past Simple — verbo + ed (regular) o una forma irregular:

I played football yesterday.

She went to school.

Did you see the movie?

---

## 3. Example

Un niño jugando al fútbol ayer.

I played football yesterday.
