## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy repasaremos much/many — cantidad en el pasado y el futuro.
There were many people at the park.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

many + un sustantivo plural:
many people, many books

much + un sustantivo singular que no se puede contar:
much time, much water

There were many people at the park.

---
