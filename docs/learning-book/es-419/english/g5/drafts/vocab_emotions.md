## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos sobre emociones con Past Simple.
She felt proud after the test.

## 2. Simple explanation

felt — la forma en pasado de feel:

She felt proud after the test.

He felt happy yesterday.

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "They felt tired after the game."

"Feel" se convierte en "felt" en el pasado, porque es un verbo irregular.

"Tired" describe cómo se sintieron; "after the game" nos dice cuándo.

Así que la oración completa es: They felt tired after the game.
