## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos Modales — can, must, have to.
You must wear a helmet.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

can — habilidad o permiso:
I can swim.

must y have to — una obligación:
You must wear a helmet.

---

## 3. Example

Un ciclista — un casco.

You must wear a helmet.

---

## 4. Let's solve together

## 5. Try it yourself

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "We have to arrive on time."

have to = una obligación (una alternativa a must).

we have to + verbo (sin un "to" extra).

Así que la oración completa es: We have to arrive on time.
