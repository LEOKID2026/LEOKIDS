## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos sobre viajes, con una primera mirada al Present Perfect — have + past participle.
We have visited three countries so far.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

have visited — describe algo hasta ahora, sin decir exactamente cuándo:

We have visited three countries so far.

I have never flown alone.

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "They have already visited five countries this year."

Present Perfect: have + el past participle (visited).

"Already" significa antes de ahora; "this year" significa hasta ahora.

Así que la oración completa es: They have already visited five countries this year.
