## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos should, might y could — no solo repetir can/must.
You should ask the teacher for help.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

should — consejo; might — tal vez; could — una posibilidad:

You should ask the teacher for help.

It might rain later.

We could meet after school.
