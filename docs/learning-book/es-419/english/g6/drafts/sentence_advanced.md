## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy armaremos oraciones complejas de 8 a 10 palabras con While y Past Continuous.
While I was studying, my sister was listening to music.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

While + Past Continuous (dos acciones al mismo tiempo):

While I was studying, my sister was listening to music.

While they were playing outside, it started to rain.

---

## 3. Example

Una tarde en casa — estudiando y música.

While I was studying, my sister was listening to music.

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "While my mother was cooking, my father was reading a book."

Dos acciones al mismo tiempo — usamos Past Continuous para ambas.

"Was cooking" y "was reading" muestran dos cosas ocurriendo juntas.

Así que la oración completa es: While my mother was cooking, my father was reading a book.
