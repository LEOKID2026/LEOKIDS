## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy repasaremos vocabulario de animales con Past Continuous — una acción que estaba ocurriendo en el pasado.
The whales were swimming when we saw them.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Past Continuous — usamos was o were con un verbo + ing:

The whales were swimming when we saw them.

The dolphins were jumping near the boat.

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "The eagles were flying over the mountains when we arrived."

Una acción en progreso en el pasado usa Past Continuous: were flying.

"When we arrived" usa Past Simple, para una acción que ocurrió en un momento.

Así que la oración completa es: The eagles were flying over the mountains when we arrived.
