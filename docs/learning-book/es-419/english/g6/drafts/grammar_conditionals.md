## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos Conditionals — Tipo 0 (una regla que siempre es verdad) y Tipo 1 (un futuro posible).
If it rains, we will stay at home.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Tipo 0 — siempre verdadero: If you heat ice, it melts.

Tipo 1 — If + Present, will + verbo:
If it rains, we will stay at home.
