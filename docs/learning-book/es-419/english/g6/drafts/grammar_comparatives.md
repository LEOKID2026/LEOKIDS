## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos Superlatives — por ejemplo, the most o the best.
This is the most interesting book I have read.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Superlative — the + most + un adjetivo largo:

This is the most interesting book I have read.

She is the best player on the team.

## 6. Let's check together

Ejercicio: "This is the funniest film I have watched."

"Funny" es un adjetivo corto — el superlativo es the + funniest (cambia y por i y agrega est).

"I have watched" usa Present Perfect.

Así que la oración completa es: This is the funniest film I have watched.
