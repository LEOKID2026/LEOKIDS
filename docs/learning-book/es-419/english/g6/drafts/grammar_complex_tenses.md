## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy aprenderemos Past Continuous junto con Past Simple, y una breve introducción al Present Perfect.
I was reading when my friend called.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Past Continuous — usamos was o were con un verbo + ing, para una acción que estaba en progreso:
I was reading when my friend called.

Past Simple — called (un evento corto):
She was cooking when the phone rang.

Present Perfect (una introducción): I have visited London.

---
