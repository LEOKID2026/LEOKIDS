## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy reforzaremos los patrones I am, You are — y agregaremos He is y She is.
Ya aprendimos I am — ahora agreguemos más pronombres.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

Patrones:
I am — yo
You are — tú
He is — él
She is — ella
Ejemplo:
She is happy.
She is happy.

---

## 3. Example

She is happy.
She is happy.
