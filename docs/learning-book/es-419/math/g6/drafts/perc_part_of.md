## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy vamos a aprender el porcentaje de una cantidad.
40% de 250.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

40% = 40/100 = 0.4
40% de 250 = 0.4 × 250 = 100
O: 250 × 40 ÷ 100 = 100

---

## 3. Visual / concrete example

25% de 80 = 20

---

## 4. Let's solve together

40% de 250 = ?
250 × 40 = 10,000
10,000 ÷ 100 = 100
100.

## 6. Common mistake — watch out!

Calcula: 30% de 150
150 × 30 = 4,500
4,500 ÷ 100 = 45
Comprueba: 45 son 30 partes de 100 de 150
La respuesta: 45
