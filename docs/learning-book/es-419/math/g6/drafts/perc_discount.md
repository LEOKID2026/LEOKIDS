## 1. What are we learning?

Hoy vamos a aprender el descuento porcentual.
Precio 180 shekels, 20% de descuento.

---

## 2. Simple explanation

20% de 180 = 180 × 20 ÷ 100 = 36 shekels de descuento.
Precio final = 180 − 36 = 144 shekels

---

## 3. Visual / concrete example

15% de 200 = 30 → 200 − 30 = 170

## 6. Common mistake — watch out!

Precio 250 shekels, 10% de descuento
Descuento = 250 × 10 ÷ 100 = 25 shekels.
Precio final = 250 − 25 = 225 shekels.
La respuesta: 225 shekels
