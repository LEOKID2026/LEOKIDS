## 1. Apa yang kita pelajari?

Hari ini kamu akan belajar Past Simple.
I played football yesterday.

---

## 2. Penjelasan sederhana

Past Simple — verb + ed (regular) atau irregular form:

I played football yesterday.

She went to school.

Melakukan kamu lihat movie?

---

## 3. Contoh

anak bermain football yesterday.

I played football yesterday.

---

## 4. Mari kita selesaikan bersama

Pertanyaan: Bagaimana kamu mengucapkan ini?

I played football yesterday

Jawaban: AKU played football yesterday

---

## 5. Coba sendiri

Pagi — team bermain basketball.

We played basketball in the morning.

Coba sendiri.
Di halaman berikutnya kita akan memeriksa jawaban dan cara mendapatkannya bersama.

---

## 6. Mari kita periksa bersama

Latihan: "Kita played basketball di pagi."

action yang selesai di past uses Past Simple: main menjadi played.

"Di pagi" tells us ketika.

Jadi penuh kalimat adalah: Kita played basketball di pagi.

---

## 7. Mari berlatih!

Sekarang kamu akan bangun kalimat di Past Simple — regular dan irregular verbs.
Di latihan, kamu akan temukan: AKU played football yesterday.
