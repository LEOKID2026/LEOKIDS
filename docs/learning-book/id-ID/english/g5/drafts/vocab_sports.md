## 6. Mari kita periksa bersama

Latihan: " footballer scored dua goals di permainan."

regular verb di past: score menjadi scored.

"Dua goals" tells us apa dia scored; "di permainan" tells us di mana.

Jadi penuh kalimat adalah: footballer scored dua goals di permainan.
